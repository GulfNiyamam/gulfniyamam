💡 Salik Quick Facts 2026:

• Per gate charge: AED 4

• Daily cap: AED 24 (6 gates/day maximum charge)

• Weekend (Fri-Sat): Same charges apply

• Recharge: app, website, petrol stations

• Negative balance fine: AED 50 + outstanding amount
